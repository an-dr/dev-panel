DevPanel 0.11.0 shows your repositories in their groups, lets you drag them into your own order, and turns the Needs attention totals into tabs.

- **Groups:** Repositories shows a section per group, with Favorites as a shelf on top and Ungrouped last. Groups and Favorites on top can each be turned off, and a section folds shut from its header.
- **Your own order:** drag a card to another place, or into another group's section, and the other cards slide aside; the list switches to the manual sort. The A–Z button on a section sorts it by name. Dropping a card in Favorites marks it a favorite and keeps its group; a favorite is reordered there but never dragged out, and its star removes it.
- **One toolbar line:** the All, Attention, Uncommitted, Behind, Unpushed and Clean tabs are gone from Repositories, Favorites and the group pages, leaving the views and sorting on one line.
- **Attention tabs:** the coloured totals on Needs attention are its tabs; the open one is ringed and narrows the list to uncommitted, behind or unpushed repositories in place.
- **Kept with the workspace:** the group options, folded sections and the manual order are saved in the workspace settings, so they follow it to another machine.
