DevPanel 0.6.0 lets you arrange the sidebar your way.

- **Drag repositories into place.** Drop a card, or a row in the list view, on Favorites to pin it, or on a group to move it there.
- **Sort the sidebar.** Drag Overview, Favorites, Repositories and Activity into any order, for example Favorites on top, and drag groups to order them. Settings can reset the order.
- **Choose the start page.** Settings → Start on picks where DevPanel opens: the page you were on, as before, or Overview, Favorites, Repositories, Activity or any group.
